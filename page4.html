The Doorman specializes in disruption, positioning, and controlling the battlefield. His powerful shotgun-like weapon rewards accurate shots, while his abilities let him disorient enemies, forcibly move them with his luggage cart, and create doors that can completely change how players move around the map. His ultimate can even send an enemy to the Baroness Hotel, temporarily removing them from the fight. The Doorman is ideal for players who enjoy outsmarting opponents and manipulating their positioning rather than simply dealing damage.
