Victor is a durable brawler who turns taking damage into an advantage. His Pain Battery stores damage he receives before releasing it back at enemies, while Jumpstart sacrifices some of his own health for increased regeneration and mobility. His Aura of Suffering lets him continuously damage nearby enemies, and even being killed isn't necessarily the end of the fight thanks to Shocking Reanimation. Victor is built for players who aren't afraid to take damage and want to stay in the middle of a fight for as long as possible.
