Deadlock is a third-person multiplayer hero shooter developed by Valve. Set in a mysterious, supernatural version of 1930s New York, players choose from a diverse cast of heroes, each with unique abilities and playstyles.

The game combines fast-paced shooting, hero abilities, and strategic lane-based gameplay. Teams must work together to push their lanes, defeat enemies, and destroy the opposing team's base.

With its unique mix of action, strategy, and urban fantasy, Deadlock offers a gameplay experience that stands apart from traditional shooters.
